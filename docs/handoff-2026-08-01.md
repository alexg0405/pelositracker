## The central finding

Measured twice, on two independent lanes, using the application's own
`_dry_segment_settlement_value` against `event_outcomes` (which carries named
home/away teams, so orientation is unambiguous):

**The entry model beats the market. The hard stop destroys the result.**

Win rate against the price paid (price = market-implied probability):

| Lane | Events | Moneyline | Spread | Giveback vs holding | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Live | 30 | +18.2pp | +17.6pp | **+88.8%** | +40.9% to +127.2% |
| Dry | 18 | +14.0pp | +6.3pp | **+34.6%** | +3.2% to +66.8% |

Dry run by exit reason: `hard_stop_loss` 139 trades, actual −$404, held +$372,
**giveback $776**. Every profit-side exit (`model_reversal`,
`profit_lock_after_edge_decay`, `trailing_profit_lock`) was positive or neutral.

Secondary: dry run was **+$22 gross and −$262 net**; estimated fees were $285,
5.7% of stake, on $5,008 of stake against a $100 allocation. Median hold time
**3.8 minutes**, 62% under five minutes. The churn is fatal independent of edge.

### What did not work

not engage**: dry 31 of 37 stops still `immediate`, live 19 of 20. Bypass
reasons are dominated by *"usable MLB inning state is unavailable"*. Do not rely
on it. Widen the stop directly instead.

## Corrections made during the session

Recorded so they are not repeated:

- "Two thirds of the loss is fees" was arithmetically right but the inference
  was wrong; with a supposed +29.8% gross edge, fees were not what turned a
  winner into a loser.
- "Edge realization −17%" was a mean of per-position ratios, meaningless with
  binary payoffs. Stake-weighted points the other way.
- The fee-aware entry gate (`fee_edge_margin`, implemented, default off) would
  have removed **zero** historical trades. It is a guardrail, not a fix.
- Per-line thresholds are **not** justified: moneyline gap minus spread gap is
  −0.7pp with a 95% interval of −16.5pp to +18.5pp. Use global thresholds and
  per-line *authorization*.
